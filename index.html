ABOUT

Hey! You've made it to my webpage! I'm glad you're here, user. ( ^ u ^ ) It's kinda clunky and rough but it'll do for now as I keep constructing this. I've got big plans for this site. Oh right, who's talking to you right now? I am Rubo Mirukawa, an online web novelist inspired by light novels, anime, fantasy fiction, visual novels, and metric tonnes of internet nonsense. I wanted to create a one-stop shop and/or home for most if not all of my IPs, half-assed ideas, story concepts, and works in progress. Because it's high time they live somewhere that isn't my mind palace.

I usually find myself writing stories about magic, love, the magic of love, strange worlds, unstable people, strange people, unstable worlds, isekai nonsense, and protagonists barely holding themselves together. There are also plots which are including but not limited to Isekai, rom-coms, comedies, psych comedies, strictly romance, strictly come dancing, coming of age, plots around newfound purposes granted to a flock of young, dilapidated has-beens, power fantasy nonsense, power fantasy absenteeism, I wrote a western at one point, feudal buccaneering, anything medieval, cheap jabs, cheaper laughs, espers of the felinic kind, the one that got away, the one that remained, antediluvian santa tries to get with the feudal times, sexytime, android slice of life, the pulpiest fiction of any narrative orange ever, oracles, john isekai, sentient gharials, honey I've lost my keys again, lagging at the terminal, portaled through the modal node network, seraphic slice of life, baseball... just baseball, incurable foresight, ten thousand bees, george got too curious, who let the pandas out?, The Capragonic Order, disbarred goddess, fogless mirror, a flock of seagulls, who ordered this?, dread and/or vexation, anything meta, isekai with a twist, isekai with a twist AND a double cross, isekai and a twist with a double cross but that double cross is actually part of the hero's plan... You get the idea. Yay for eccentricities, huh? Anyway, despite how bleak things look now, I aim to give this site several oomphs over the next little while. Thank you for being here. 1love 4all, - rubo m.
